# Sources

Every fact on the site should trace back to a source listed here. This file is used for
content review, testing (link checks) and thesis writing.

How to use it:
- Add a source when you use it for content, with the page/topic it supports.
- `Checked` = date the information was last confirmed on the source page.
- The same URL also goes into the topic's `source` field in the content JSON.
- A topic has one `source` (the "Official source" button). When its facts come from more than
  one page, the others are listed here and usually also appear in the topic's `links`.

## Team experience (not official)
Practical tips from the team, worded as "may" / "often". Re-check with newcomers during testing.

- Living: Finding a rental apartment: private landlords may want to meet in person; private rentals can be cheaper than Lahden Talot.
- Living: Money and bank account: the first free bank appointment can be weeks or months away; Helsinki branches sometimes have earlier times.
- Living: Second-hand shops: student groups give away items at the start and end of a semester.
